# Long-run research layer -- 2016-01-01 to 2026-10-04

Generated by `python -m desk.run` from the committed history stores in `data/history/` (Coin Metrics Community API; Binance public data archive for prices Coin Metrics does not publish). Method and reasoning: ARCHITECTURE.md, section 13. Nothing here is investment advice.

## Point-in-time universe

- 373 candidates from a rule-based scan; 91 priced by Coin Metrics, 183 by the Binance archive, 99 with volume but no usable price (`longrun_candidates.csv`).
- Monthly rebalance; eligible = at least 90 days of prices, trading on the previous day, median reported volume over 90 days of at least $1M, top 20 by that median. All inputs use data strictly before the rebalance date.
- Eligible assets per month: 1 to 20 (median 20); the universe first reaches 20 names on 2017-12-01.
- 108 assets were eligible at some point; 19 of them later stopped trading (AION, BTG, BTT, EOS, FTM, GNT, LOOM, LUNA, MAID, MATIC, MCO, NAS, REP, RNDR, SXP, VTC, WAVES, WTC, YFII). They stay in the returns for every day they traded.
- Exits forced by a held asset's death: 1 in the inverse-vol run (`longrun_exits.csv`).
